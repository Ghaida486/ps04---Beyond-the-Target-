## Scope of the remaining judgment

These checks establish repository contents, artifact consistency, replay behavior and recorded publication facts. They do not predict a grade, independently verify every publisher's underlying factual account, or certify every name's pronunciation. The student confirmed playback and article access; automated final-audio recognition differences are retained as listening flags. The student must be able to explain the design and the AI-assisted reflection.
