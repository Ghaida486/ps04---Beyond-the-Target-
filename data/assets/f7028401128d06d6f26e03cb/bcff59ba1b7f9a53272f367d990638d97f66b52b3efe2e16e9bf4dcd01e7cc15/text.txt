Any archer who is a member of a national archery federation recognised by World Archery may participate in the Indoor World Series.

Archers at the Indoor World Series shoot over a distance of 18 metres at smaller versions of the traditional five-colour target face with 10 concentric scoring rings, measuring 40 centimetres in diameter.

As the name suggests, the competition is made up of several events at 18 metres that any archer in the world can enter, giving aspiring amateurs the chance to compete alongside the very best.

Athletes at the Indoor Archery World Series compete in recurve , compound or barebow competitions, which correspond to the type of bow they use.

Recurve archers shoot at a target face measuring 40 centimetres in diameter, with a 10-ring measuring 4 centimetres in diameter.
Compound archers shoot at a target face with identical proportions to the recurve face, except the 10-ring is halved in size, measuring 2 centimetres in diameter.

The top 16 archers in each of the four elite categories – recurve men, recurve women, compound men and compound women – at the end of the ranking season will qualify for the Finals.

There are two ranking lists for the 2027 Indoor World Series: elite and youth. The open ranking, which was part of the 2026 system, has been removed for 2027.
The elite ranking is based on points awarded according to final placing at each stage, with each athlete’s best three results across the season counting towards their ranking. It covers four categories: recurve men, recurve women, compound men and compound women. The top 16 archers in each category at the end of the ranking season qualify for the Finals in Las Vegas.
