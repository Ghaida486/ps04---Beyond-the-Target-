Promising Nurisa Dian Ashrifah made history on Thursday, winning Indonesia’s first Asian Games gold medal in archery with victory in the compound women’s individual event.
The 20-year-old edged the Philippines’ Amaya Amparo Cojuangco 139-137 in a thrilling final after the archers were level on 110 following four ends at the Okazaki Chuo Sogo Park Multipurpose Square in Aichi Prefecture.
