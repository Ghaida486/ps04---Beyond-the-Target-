For this study, I created mathematically a few sets of arrows with varying thicknesses of the inner aluminium layer, ranging from 0 (none) to 18 thousandths of an inch. I used a constant internal shaft diameter of 3.2 mm and selected the outside diameter of the carbon fibre composite material to give the desired arrow spine. For all of the arrow shafts, I used a constant carbon fibre composite Young’s Modulus (that is, a constant stiffness).

I used a bow with a peak draw force of 200 N (45 pounds) and stored energy of 81 J for this chart.

In every case, the addition of an aluminium layer led to less wind drift.
